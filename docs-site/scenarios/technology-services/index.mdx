Technology service providers operate across incidents, change, assets, compliance, delivery, and customer success. These scenarios show how to model those operations with clear object types and actions.



<!-- four-step-index-note:start -->
## Four-Step Pattern

Every detailed scenario in this library is now framed as the same four-step application pattern:

1. Capture demand and context.
2. Prepare the decision.
3. Execute and collaborate.
4. Resolve, explain, and improve.

Each scenario page calls out the goal of each step, the data needed to achieve it, the AI support that improves the experience, the business impact, and whether the current EAI delivery model is sufficient or needs extension.

## Typical Building Blocks

- Assets, identities, tickets, changes, releases, programs, and service contracts
- Operational governance for SLAs, approvals, incidents, and renewal risk
- Document workflows for policies, reports, implementation packs, and runbooks
- AI support for triage, summarisation, classification, and decision support
