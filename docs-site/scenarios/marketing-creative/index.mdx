Marketing and creative teams need systems that connect briefs, assets, approvals, channels, performance, and client reporting. These scenarios show how to structure those workflows in a vertical application.



<!-- four-step-index-note:start -->
## Four-Step Pattern

Every detailed scenario in this library is now framed as the same four-step application pattern:

1. Capture demand and context.
2. Prepare the decision.
3. Execute and collaborate.
4. Resolve, explain, and improve.

Each scenario page calls out the goal of each step, the data needed to achieve it, the AI support that improves the experience, the business impact, and whether the current EAI delivery model is sufficient or needs extension.

## Typical Building Blocks

- Campaigns, briefs, audiences, assets, approvals, and performance objects
- Cross-functional workflows for creative, media, analytics, and compliance
- Document-heavy intake for briefs, research, and creative packs
- AI support for summarisation, classification, optimisation, and insight extraction
