Business services teams run high-volume workflows that combine intake, coordination, delivery, and client reporting. These scenarios show how to model those operating systems with the EAI CLI.



<!-- four-step-index-note:start -->
## Four-Step Pattern

Every detailed scenario in this library is now framed as the same four-step application pattern:

1. Capture demand and context.
2. Prepare the decision.
3. Execute and collaborate.
4. Resolve, explain, and improve.

Each scenario page calls out the goal of each step, the data needed to achieve it, the AI support that improves the experience, the business impact, and whether the current EAI delivery model is sufficient or needs extension.

## Typical Building Blocks

- Requests, engagements, candidates, programs, deliverables, and service tickets
- Multi-team delivery with approvals, SLAs, milestones, and financial controls
- Document workflows for briefs, reports, assessments, and client packs
- AI support for intake, summaries, routing, and quality review
