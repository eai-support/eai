Education platforms often combine student records, course workflows, and document-heavy review processes. These scenarios show how to build those systems with clear schema-driven models.



<!-- four-step-index-note:start -->
## Four-Step Pattern

Every detailed scenario in this library is now framed as the same four-step application pattern:

1. Capture demand and context.
2. Prepare the decision.
3. Execute and collaborate.
4. Resolve, explain, and improve.

Each scenario page calls out the goal of each step, the data needed to achieve it, the AI support that improves the experience, the business impact, and whether the current EAI delivery model is sufficient or needs extension.

## Typical Education Building Blocks

- Students, courses, applications, assessments, and grant records
- Faculty, campus, or institution-level tenant separation
- Document workflows for submissions, transcripts, and compliance evidence
- AI support for summarisation, analytics, and document classification
