## Typical Building Blocks

- Matters, contracts, evidence, filings, reviews, deadlines, and billing records
- Role-sensitive workflows across client service, review, approvals, and compliance
- Document-heavy operations for redlines, filings, productions, and opinions
- AI support for summarisation, classification, issue spotting, and draft review
