## Typical Building Blocks

- Assets, identities, tickets, changes, releases, programs, and service contracts
- Operational governance for SLAs, approvals, incidents, and renewal risk
- Document workflows for policies, reports, implementation packs, and runbooks
- AI support for triage, summarisation, classification, and decision support
