## Typical Building Blocks

- Requisitions, suppliers, orders, shipments, routes, exceptions, and compliance records
- Operational workflows across procurement, transport, warehousing, and service recovery
- Document workflows for manifests, RFx packs, proofs, and supplier evidence
- AI support for triage, ETA reasoning, summarisation, and exception resolution
