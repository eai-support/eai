## Typical Building Blocks

- Properties, deals, drawings, bids, inspections, vendors, and closing records
- Multi-party workflows for owners, buyers, lenders, consultants, and contractors
- Document workflows for contracts, drawings, reports, and settlement packs
- AI support for summarisation, classification, issue routing, and review preparation
