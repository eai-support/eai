## Business Problem

Capital project bidding teams coordinate bid packages, clarifications, addenda, pricing versions, risk reviews, interviews, and award handovers. Each bid cycle involves many approvals and a large evidence trail.

## Required Object Model (22 object types)

This scenario needs more than 20 object types because it spans intake, delivery, exceptions, governance, and reporting.

### Tender Setup

- `Opportunity` — bid opportunity
- `Client` — client organisation
- `Site` — project site
- `RequirementSet` — client requirements
- `BidPackage` — bid package
- `ScopeSection` — scope section
- `Estimator` — assigned estimator
- `SupplierBid` — supplier bid

### Commercial Assembly

- `SubcontractorBid` — subcontractor bid
- `ClarificationLog` — clarification log
- `Addendum` — issued addendum
- `ScheduleOption` — schedule option
- `RiskRegister` — bid risk register
- `PricingVersion` — pricing version
- `ValueEngineeringIdea` — value-engineering item
- `ApprovalGate` — internal approval gate

### Award and Mobilisation

- `TenderSubmission` — submitted tender
- `InterviewRound` — post-submission interview
- `AwardNotice` — award notice
- `ContractDraft` — contract draft
- `MobilizationPlan` — mobilisation plan
- `HandoverChecklist` — handover checklist

## AI and Document Opportunities

- Classify tender documents and clarifications into the right bid package and scope section.
- Summarise pricing changes and top bid risks before executive approval gates.
- Generate mobilisation checklists from the final bid, contract draft, and award terms.

## Why This Scenario Is High-Value

Winning capital projects depends on bid discipline, not just pricing. This scenario helps teams manage the full commercial and operational lifecycle from tender to handover.
